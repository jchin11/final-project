5 years of Destiny 2

My experience told by screenshots

Just last month I found out that I would snap a duplicate screenshot of the game whenever I would use snipping tool. This meant that I had a 7GB folder that consisted of years upon years of screenshots I had unknowingly taken. I've been playing this game for ~25% of my life, so I find it neat that I can see my experience with this game unfold over simple screenshots. There are many moments in this collection that would bring back memories I'd never have unearthed before, so I'd imagine that some may be amusing without context. The last one was taken just yesterday, so I can still update this page the more I play the game, which I find incredible
